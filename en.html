Privacy Policy

Apps: Expecto: Parent (parent app) and Expecto: Kids (child phone app) Version: 1 · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy is the privacy notice under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, articles 13-14) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, article 10).

1. Data controller

  • Name: Atakan Küpçüoğlu
  • Address: Sakarya, Türkiye
  • Contact email: expecto.support@gmail.com
  • Website: https://kupcuogluatakan.github.io/expecto-legal/

2. Who it is for

Expecto is a parent tool. Expecto: Parent is used by parents or guardians aged 18 or over. Expecto: Kids is installed by the parent, with the parent's knowledge, on their own child's phone.

3. What personal data is processed

Parent (Expecto: Parent)

  • With email and password sign-in: account details (the password is held by Firebase Authentication and is not visible to us).
  • With Google sign-in: Google account name, email address and account ID.
  • Subscription: purchase history and subscription status, linked to the family ID, are kept by RevenueCat, Inc. (a processor). No card data is collected; Google Play handles payment.
  • Family data: names and ages entered for children, app rules, schedules, safe zones (geofences), family time zone, invite and pairing codes.

Child phone (Expecto: Kids)

  • List of installed apps.
  • Time used per app.
  • Protection and permission states (for example whether accessibility, usage access or location permission is on).
  • Battery level.
  • Location history and live location.
  • Location when the SOS button is pressed.
  • Notes the child types in time requests.

The child phone signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously; no account, email or phone number is asked for the child.

Map

Expecto: Parent loads the map from tiles.openfreemap.org. To send the map images, this server sees the parent phone's IP address and the map area being viewed.

4. Purposes

Data is processed only to provide the app's service: - show usage reports to the parent, - apply the time limits and app rules the parent sets on the child phone, - show the child's location, location history and safe-zone entries and exits, - alert the parent about SOS, time requests and protection problems (for example a permission turned off), - account and family management (sign-in, pairing, inviting a second parent, account deletion).

No ads. Data is not sold. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used.

QR scanning on the child phone uses Google's ML Kit code scanner (Google Play services). According to Google's published ML Kit data disclosure, it sends device and app information, identifiers and performance metrics to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics; we do not receive this data.

5. Legal basis

  • GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and KVKK art. 5(2)(c): performance of the service (contract) with the parent.
  • GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and KVKK art. 5(1) and 9: the parent's explicit consent, including for the transfer abroad and the child's location data. Consent is asked in the parent app before sign-in.
  • The child's data is processed with the consent and on the instruction of the parent as the child's guardian.

6. Where data is stored and transfer abroad

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud; Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, European Union). For users in Türkiye this is a transfer of personal data abroad, based on the parent's explicit consent. Google acts as a service provider (processor). For subscription data we also use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as a processor; this transfer is likewise based on the parent's explicit consent.

Map images are requested from tiles.openfreemap.org (see section 3).

7. Retention

Data Period
Location history 30 days
SOS location 30 days
Usage and report data 90 days
Account and family data (rules, zones, child name/age) Until the parent deletes it

The parent can delete a child (with all of that child's data) or their own account at any time in Family settings in Expecto: Parent. When the family owner deletes their account, all children's data in that family is deleted too.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR articles 15-22 and KVKK article 11 you have the right to know whether your data is processed, to get information and a copy, to know the purpose and the recipients, to have incomplete or wrong data corrected, to have data erased, to restrict processing, to object, to data portability, to object to the result of automated processing, and to claim compensation for damage. You can withdraw your consent at any time (deleting your account is enough).

Send requests to expecto.support@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days at the latest. You can also complain to your EU data protection authority or to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Firestore security rules let only the parents of a family and its paired child phone access that family's data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the parent app shows the new version and asks for consent again before continuing.
